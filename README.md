# Policy Review Lab

## Overview

This project demonstrates a basic Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) activity: reviewing an information security policy and identifying opportunities for improvement.

The objective was to assess an Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), identify security gaps, evaluate associated risks and provide recommendations aligned with good information security practices.

---

## Project Objectives

- Review an existing policy
- Identify missing security requirements
- Assess potential risks
- Recommend corrective actions
- Demonstrate policy governance skills

---

## Documents Included

### 1. Acceptable Use Policy

A simplified Acceptable Use Policy created for review purposes.

The policy defines basic expectations for employees using company systems and information resources.

### 2. Policy Review Report

A formal review identifying security gaps and improvement opportunities.

The report includes:

- Executive Summary
- Findings
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Priority Ratings
- Conclusion

---

## Findings Identified

### Finding 1 – Missing MFA Requirements

Risk:
Account compromise through stolen credentials.

Recommendation:
Implement Multi-Factor Authentication requirements.

Priority:
High

---

### Finding 2 – Missing Password Requirements

Risk:
Weak passwords increase the likelihood of unauthorized access.

Recommendation:
Define password complexity and management requirements.

Priority:
High

---

### Finding 3 – Missing Remote Working Controls

Risk:
Unauthorized access from unmanaged locations.

Recommendation:
Introduce remote working security requirements.

Priority:
Medium

---

### Finding 4 – Missing Incident Reporting Requirements

Risk:
Delayed detection and response to security incidents.

Recommendation:
Define incident reporting responsibilities and escalation procedures.

Priority:
Medium

---

### Finding 5 – Missing Data Classification Guidance

Risk:
Sensitive information may be handled incorrectly.

Recommendation:
Introduce data classification requirements and handling guidance.

Priority:
Medium
